PRIVACY AND SECURITY

Privacy controls you can see and understand.

Flowr reduces unwanted tracking at the browser layer and gives you direct control over history, permissions and credentials.

Ad and tracker blocking

Flowr checks network requests against maintained blocking rules before known advertising, analytics, tracking and cryptomining endpoints load. First-party Tieddr services are separated from third-party advertising rules so account and product links continue to work.

Safer connections and permissions

HTTPS upgrades prefer encrypted connections when a website supports them. Per-site controls let you review access to the camera, microphone, location, notifications, pop-ups and automatic redirects instead of accepting one global choice.

Private history controls

Private browsing avoids adding ordinary visits to saved history. Optional automatic clearing can remove history on exit or for selected sites, while history and browser locks can be protected through Tieddr Vault.

Passwords through Tieddr Vault

Vault integration is designed to keep saved credentials encrypted and make autofill available inside Flowr after you unlock your Tieddr account. Clipboard clearing and automatic locking reduce the time sensitive information remains exposed.

What browser privacy cannot promise

A browser cannot make activity invisible to every operating system component, employer-managed device, network administrator or internet provider. Encrypted HTTPS connections protect page contents in transit, but network operators can still observe connection metadata such as destination addresses. Flowr describes these limits plainly instead of promising anonymity it cannot guarantee.

For stronger network privacy: combine Flowr with trustworthy encrypted DNS or a reputable VPN, keep the operating system updated, and avoid installing extensions you do not trust.
