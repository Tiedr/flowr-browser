Why we recommend Flowr for a calmer web

The best browser is the one that helps you finish what you opened it to do. Flowr is designed around that simple idea.

Less browser, more page

Flowr keeps its interface restrained so the page remains the main event. Tabs, navigation and tools are close when you need them, while themes and start-page backgrounds let the browser feel personal without becoming noisy.

Your Tieddr tools belong together

Flowr connects directly to Tieddr Space, Vault and Mavis. That means bookmarks and notes can stay near your work, credentials can be reached through the built-in Vault surface, and assistance is available without assembling a patchwork of unrelated tools.

Privacy controls you can understand

Tracker blocking, private windows, HTTPS controls, per-site permissions and operating-system-protected credential storage are visible settings—not hidden promises. No browser can make the web risk-free, but good defaults and understandable controls make a meaningful difference.

Our recommendation: choose Flowr if you value a calm interface and already use—or want to use—the Tieddr ecosystem. If cross-platform maturity or a huge extension ecosystem is your first priority, compare the alternatives before switching.

A young browser with a clear direction

Flowr is currently available for Windows, with macOS coming soon. It is earlier in its journey than the largest browsers, so we are building in public and prioritizing reliability, honest release notes and a focused experience over feature count.
