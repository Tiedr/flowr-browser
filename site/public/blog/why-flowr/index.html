Why choose Flowr for a calmer web?

The best browser is the one that helps you finish what you opened it to do. Flowr is designed around that simple idea.

Less browser, more page

Flowr keeps its interface restrained so the page remains the main event. Tabs, navigation and tools are close when you need them, while themes and start-page backgrounds let the browser feel personal without becoming noisy.

Tabs built around real work

Split tabs keep two related pages together, while collapsible tab groups contain larger projects without scattering them across the tab strip. Flowr is designed to preserve page state as you organise, move and revisit tabs.

Your Tieddr tools belong together

Flowr connects directly to Tieddr Space, Vault and Mavis. Bookmarks and notes can stay near your work, credentials can be reached through the built-in Vault surface, and assistance remains available in the browser side panel.

Privacy controls you can understand

Tracker blocking, private windows, HTTPS controls, per-site permissions and operating-system-protected credential storage are visible settings—not hidden promises. No browser can make the web risk-free or conceal traffic from every network, but good defaults and understandable controls make a meaningful difference.

Our recommendation: choose Flowr if you value a calm interface and already use—or want to use—the Tieddr ecosystem. Keep your current browser installed while you test Flowr with your own daily sites.

Available across desktop platforms

Flowr is available for Windows, macOS and Linux. It is earlier in its journey than the largest browsers, so Tieddr is building it in public and prioritising reliability, honest release notes and a focused experience over feature count.
