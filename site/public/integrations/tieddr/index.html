Vault, Space and Mavis stay inside your flow.

Sign in once and carry your Tieddr identity across browser surfaces without replacing your real name or profile photo with guesses.

Unlock passwords with Vault

Import supported passwords into Tieddr Vault after sign-in, then use Vault-powered suggestions and autofill across Flowr. Browser and history locks can use the same protected account surface.

Save pages and bookmarks to Space

Send a page, highlighted passage or note to Tieddr Space from the browser. Imported bookmarks remain available in Flowr and can be connected to your account after you choose what to keep.

Ask Mavis without covering the page

Mavis opens in a docked side panel that reduces the page viewport instead of floating above website controls. It uses the Mavis identity and account requirement consistently across Flowr and Tieddr Space.

Your session should survive updates

Flowr stores the browser-wide Tieddr session in the user profile so reinstalling or applying an update does not deliberately sign you out. You may still need to authenticate again after an explicit sign-out, security reset or expired server session.
