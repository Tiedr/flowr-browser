TAB WORKSPACES

Two ways to bring related tabs together.

Split tabs pair two live pages inside one tab. Tab groups organise a larger set and collapse them behind one clear name.

Split view belongs to the paired tabs

A Flowr split is not a permanent division across every tab. It combines the two selected pages into one paired tab, keeps both pages live, and remains intact while you visit other tabs. Use the split control to swap sides, resize the divider or end the pairing.

Drag one tab onto another to group

Drop a tab directly over another tab to create a group. Dragging horizontally reorders tabs, while moving a tab out of the tab strip transfers it into a new window. Links opened from a grouped page stay next to their parent and inside the same group.

Collapse the whole project

Click the group name to hide or reveal its tabs. The group remains one contiguous unit in the tab strip, carries its chosen name and colour, and can be moved without scattering its members.

Suggestions, never forced organisation

When Flowr notices repeated switching between the same two pages, it can suggest a split. When several tabs share a domain or topic, it can suggest a group. Nothing changes until you accept the suggestion.

Previews without switching

Hover a normal tab to see its latest captured page preview. Hover a paired tab to see both sides, their titles and their URLs without activating the split first.
