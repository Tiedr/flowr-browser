# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Flow Browser is for people who want a lightweight desktop browser that feels calm, premium, and fast while still giving them practical control over tabs, history, bookmarks, downloads, profiles, themes, and extensions. Users are in a daily browsing workflow: searching, reading, downloading files, saving pages, switching contexts, and tuning the app to feel personal without becoming busy.

## Product Purpose

Flow Browser exists to provide a focused Electron browser with a refined native-app feel and enough utility to be genuinely usable as a daily driver. Success means the app launches reliably, navigation feels predictable, browser data is easy to manage, and advanced features such as themes and Chrome-compatible unpacked extensions are discoverable without cluttering the primary browsing surface.

## Brand Personality

Calm, premium, precise. Flow should feel polished and trustworthy, closer to Safari's restraint than a power-user cockpit, while still exposing real tools when the user asks for them.

## Anti-references

Avoid noisy power-browser chrome, crowded toolbars, novelty controls, harsh high-saturation themes, generic glass-heavy mockups, and settings pages that feel like unfinished developer panels. The app should not feel like a themed Chromium clone or a demo shell with disconnected menus.

## Design Principles

1. Keep browsing primary: navigation, tabs, and page content stay visually quiet and fast.
2. Reveal utility on demand: menus, dialogs, and management pages give depth without crowding the toolbar.
3. Make state visible: downloads, bookmarks, profile, extension status, and theme choices should always have clear feedback.
4. Prefer native familiarity: standard browser actions should behave as users expect.
5. Polish every empty state: pages with no data should still explain what can happen next.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target strong keyboard and screen-reader basics for app UI, high-contrast readable text, clear focus states, and reduced-motion-friendly interactions. Themes should preserve legibility and avoid relying on color alone for critical state.
